For the second year, I participated to the global music challenge: Jamuary.

It is a loose organization and everyone sets their own goals. In my case,
I wanted to have a better visual support for my jams and also improve on
the quality of the songs and performances. Some songs were mostly written
when hitting the "record" button whereas some others were more improvised.

I'm still learning my instruments, so this event was a great opportunity for
me push new ideas.
